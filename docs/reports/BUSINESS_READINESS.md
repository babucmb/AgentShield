# Business readiness

## Hackathon MVP

The product is close to hackathon-demo readiness. Its strongest differentiator is an authorization firewall between AI recommendations and PayPal execution: AI extracts intent, but deterministic policies and explicit approval control payment.

## Initial buyer

The most specific first buyer is an AI shopping or procurement application that wants to delegate product research while retaining user-controlled payment authorization and an explanation trail.

## Commercial hypothesis

- Developer API subscription with usage-based proposal evaluations.
- Paid audit retention and policy controls for business workspaces.
- Enterprise onboarding and payment-provider integration support.

These are hypotheses, not validated pricing or demand. Customer interviews and a controlled pilot are still required.

## Business MVP gaps

- Organization isolation on every proposal and payment endpoint.
- Durable production database and backups.
- Admin policy management and approval roles.
- Operational reconciliation and alerts.
- Privacy, retention, terms, and compliance review.
- At least three pilot customers or documented discovery interviews.

## Production verdict

Not production ready. No penetration test, live operational monitoring, external customer validation, or real-money approval has been completed.
